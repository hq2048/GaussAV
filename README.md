# Viewport-Importance-Embedded 3D Gaussian Splatting Video with Spatial Audio

This is the official repository for our paper **"Viewport-Importance-Embedded 3D Gaussian Splatting Video with Spatial Audio"**, accepted for publication in **IEEE Transactions on Multimedia (TMM)**.

## 📰 News

- **Our paper has been accepted by IEEE Transactions on Multimedia (TMM)!**

## 📂 Dataset

Due to the large size of our dataset, we provide only a few representative samples in this repository.

If you would like to obtain the complete dataset, please contact us at **huqi@mail.hfut.edu.cn**.
